Help & Support
Most issues are fixed in under a minute — please try these 3 steps first.
  1. Update the extension
    Make sure you're on the latest version, then follow the quick instructions at truckbox.app/update.
  2. Logout, then login again
    When signing back in, make sure you tick the checkbox that lets us send email on your behalf — otherwise your emails won't go out.
  3. Refresh the DAT page
    Reload your DAT One tab (or just press F5) so the extension reconnects.

    Route Map

    Turn the map off to keep load details compact. The RPM stats, Copy, rate calculator and FMCSA report stay.

    Duplicate Load Filter

    Dims repeated loads (same lane, date, rate & broker) so you only act on each one once.

    Profit Calculator

    Rate minus fuel, tolls and driver pay, with your break-even, inside the load's Rate column. Turn it off and the column goes back to the way the board draws it.

    DAT's Email Button

    DAT draws its own send-email icon next to ours. It's hidden by default so there's only one button to hit — turn this on if you want both. The broker's email address stays visible either way.

    Desktop Notifications

    A small desktop pop-up when an email goes out, when a saved load matches, or when a mailbox needs reconnecting. Chrome asks for permission the first time you turn this on, and turning it off takes the permission away again.
