User Guide

Login and setup guide

Follow these steps to get started with Truck Box. Add screenshots later for visual guidance.

Step 1 — Install extension

Open the Chrome Web Store and install Truck Box. Pin it to your toolbar for easy access.

Screenshot: Chrome Web Store installation

Step 2 — Open popup

Click the Truck Box extension icon in Chrome to open the login panel.

Screenshot: Extension popup

Step 3 — Login with Google

Use the Google login button and approve the requested permissions. This allows Truck Box to send emails on your behalf.

Screenshot: Google login screen

Step 4 — Start free trial / subscription

After login, activate your 7-day free trial or choose a subscription plan.

Screenshot: Trial activation

Step 5 — Fill in your email template

Set your default subject and message body for broker emails. This saves time on every load.

Template tips

  • Keep subject short and clear (e.g., "Available: [Origin] to [Destination]")
  • Use a professional intro with your company name
  • Include MC number, phone, and lane intent
  • Make the body reusable for different loads
Screenshot: Template settings

Step 6 — Use inside DAT

Open one.dat.com, browse loads, and send emails with one click using your templates.

Screenshot: DAT with Truck Box buttons
