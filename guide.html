Step 4 — Start free trial

Step 5 — Fill in your email template

Set your default subject and message body for broker emails. This saves time on every load.

Template tips

  • Keep subject short and clear.
  • Use a professional intro with your company name.
  • Include MC number, phone, and lane intent.
  • Make the body reusable for different loads.
